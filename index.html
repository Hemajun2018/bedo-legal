Help

How do I cancel or change BeDo Pro?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BeDo. Or in BeDo: Me → BeDo Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

I bought Pro but BeDo still shows the free version.

Open Me → BeDo Pro and tap "Restore Purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

My apps don't lock.

BeDo needs Screen Time access: Me shows a card if it's off. Only one app on a phone can hold that access at a time, so another blocker app can get in the way.

How do I delete my data?

Me → Privacy & data → Delete all data. Everything lives on your phone, so deleting the app removes it too.

帮助

怎么取消或更换 BeDo Pro？

在 iPhone 上：设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅 → BeDo。也可以在 BeDo 里：我 → BeDo Pro。在当期结束前至少 24 小时取消，就不会再扣下一期。

买了 Pro，BeDo 还显示免费版？

用付款的那个 Apple ID 登录，打开「我 → BeDo Pro」，点「恢复购买」。

App 锁不上？

BeDo 需要「屏幕使用时间」权限，没开的话「我」页顶上会提醒。一台手机同一时间只能有一个 App 拿着这个权限，装了别的锁 App 可能会冲突。

怎么删除我的数据？

我 → 隐私与数据 → 删掉全部数据。所有数据都在你手机上，删掉 App 也会一起删掉。
