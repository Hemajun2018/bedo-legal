BeDo Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

BeDo helps you finish the few things you set for yourself each day: until they're done, your distracting apps stay locked. This policy explains what BeDo does with your information. The short version: your things, records, photos and recordings stay on your phone. We have no accounts, no user database, no ads and no tracking. The only data that leaves your phone is what goes to the AI service that powers Bedo, by way of a small relay of ours that keeps none of it, and only after you agree.

  • No sign-up. No account. Our only server is a relay that passes AI requests along and stores none of them.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites. We never sell your data.
  • Which apps you lock is hidden even from BeDo: iOS only gives it unreadable tokens.
  • Your Apple Health data and your recordings never leave your phone.
  • When you talk to Bedo, what you send goes through our relay to DeepSeek, an AI service, and only after you say yes.

Who we are

BeDo is made by Wuxi Entropy Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

What stays on your phone

All of the following is stored only in BeDo's storage on your device. We don't receive it and can't see it.

  • Your things and their rules: names, days, lock times, how each one counts as done, smaller versions, scripts you read from.
  • Your records: when you finished what, counts and times, notes you wrote, photos and screenshots you kept, voice recordings, your wall, weekly cards and letters.
  • Settings: Bedo's name, when your day ends, how many early unlocks you allow yourself, and similar.

BeDo does not currently sync to iCloud. Like other app data, this information may be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) according to your iOS settings.

Permissions and what they're used for

Screen Time

BeDo uses Apple's Screen Time framework to lock the apps you pick and to know when you've spent a set time in an app. iOS gives BeDo only encrypted tokens for those apps. Neither we nor BeDo can tell which apps they are, and these tokens never leave your phone.

Camera

Used to count reps (push-ups, squats and so on), to take photos you keep as proof, and to scan pages you want to read aloud. Rep counting happens on your phone in real time and the video is not saved. Text in scanned pages and pictures is read on your phone.

Microphone and speech recognition

Used to record you when a thing is done by voice, and to turn what you say into text when you talk to Bedo. Recordings are saved only on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Speech-to-text runs on your phone when your language supports it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Health

If you set a thing to finish from Health data, BeDo reads today's walking and running distance, steps, exercise minutes or mindful minutes. Read only: BeDo never writes to Health. Health data stays on your phone. It is not sent to the AI service or to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

Photos

Used when you choose a screenshot or picture to send. BeDo only sees the pictures you pick.

Notifications

All BeDo notifications are scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.

What goes to the AI service

Bedo, BeDo's companion, is powered by DeepSeek, an AI service (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research Co., Ltd.). The first time something would be sent, BeDo asks you in the chat and sends nothing unless you agree. If you don't agree, BeDo still works: you set things up yourself, checks still work, and Bedo replies with fixed lines. Photo checks aren't offered in that case.

When you've agreed, these can be sent, and only these:

Before a photo is sent, BeDo shrinks it and re-encodes it, which strips location and other camera metadata.

Requests go from your phone to a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers. It adds our DeepSeek key, passes the request to DeepSeek and streams the reply straight back. The relay doesn't log or store what you send or what comes back. To stop the service being used by anything other than BeDo, and to count your daily messages, it keeps only:

  • An anonymous device ID: the ID of a key your iPhone creates for BeDo with Apple's App Attest, plus that key's public half. It isn't linked to your name, Apple ID or any account, and it changes if you reinstall BeDo. It's deleted after 90 days without use.
  • How many requests that device ID made today (kept for about two days), and, before you start, how many in total.
  • With BeDo Pro, a fingerprint (hash) of your Apple-signed purchase record and when it expires, so the relay doesn't have to check Apple's signature every time.

As with any internet request, Cloudflare, which runs the relay, can see your IP address, and it may be passed on to DeepSeek; the relay doesn't store it. DeepSeek processes data on servers in the People's Republic of China, and how long it keeps data is set by DeepSeek's own privacy policy and terms (see deepseek.com). Bedo's replies are generated by AI and may be wrong.

Subscriptions

BeDo is sold by Apple as an auto-renewing subscription (yearly, with a free trial, or monthly) or as a one-time purchase. Apple handles payment; we never receive your payment details. To know whether your trial, subscription or purchase is active, BeDo checks Apple-signed purchase records on your phone, and sends the current one with each AI request so our relay can check Apple's signature itself. The relay keeps only a fingerprint of that record and when it expires. To stop abuse, the relay counts messages under your anonymous device ID: a few in total before you start, and a daily ceiling you won't reach in normal use. If you allow notifications, BeDo reminds you two days before a free trial ends; that reminder is scheduled on your phone. Manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Purchases are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What we receive

Never what you send to Bedo. Our relay keeps only the anonymous device ID, daily counts and Pro fingerprint described in "What goes to the AI service". Beyond that, we only receive information if you choose to send it to us, for example by email or through TestFlight feedback. Apple may also give us anonymized App Store statistics and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers. We use these only to answer you and to fix and improve BeDo.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. You can delete a thing and its records, delete everything in Me → Privacy & data → Delete all data, or delete the app. Deleting all data can't be undone. It doesn't reset your daily message count: the relay's record of your anonymous device ID goes away on its own after 90 days without use. Things already sent to DeepSeek are held under DeepSeek's terms. We can't access or delete them because we never kept them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or privacy laws in US states such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Because your data lives on your phone, you can see, change and delete it in BeDo yourself. For anything else, contact us. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent to send content to the AI service. You can withdraw it by not sending further messages and deleting your data. We rely on performing our service to you for everything that happens on your device.

Children

BeDo isn't directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age set by law in your country, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If what BeDo sends or stores changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change ships. For significant changes, BeDo will tell you in the app.

BeDo 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

BeDo 帮你把每天给自己定的几件小事做完：没做完，分心 App 就锁着。这份政策说明 BeDo 怎么处理你的信息。一句话：你的事情、记录、照片和录音都留在你的手机上。我们没有账号体系、没有用户数据库、没有广告、不做追踪。唯一离开手机的，是发给支撑 Bedo 的 AI 服务的内容（中间经过我们一个什么都不保存的中转服务），而且要你先同意。

  • 不用注册，没有账号。我们唯一的服务器是一个中转服务：只转发 AI 请求，一条都不保存。
  • 没有广告，没有统计分析 SDK，不跨 App 或网站追踪你。我们从不出售你的数据。
  • 你锁了哪些 App，连 BeDo 自己都不知道：iOS 只给它一串读不出来的标识。
  • 你的「健康」数据和录音永远不离开你的手机。
  • 你跟 Bedo 说话时，发出去的内容会经过我们的中转服务交给 AI 服务 DeepSeek，而且要你先点同意。

我们是谁

BeDo 由 Wuxi Entropy Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd（以下称「我们」）开发。联系方式见本政策末尾。

留在你手机上的

下面这些只存在你手机上 BeDo 的存储空间里。我们收不到，也看不到。

  • 你的事和规则：名字、哪几天、几点起锁、怎么算做完、保底版、朗读用的稿子。
  • 你的记录：什么时候做完了什么、个数和时长、你写的话、留下的照片和截图、录音、成就墙、周卡和来信。
  • 设置：Bedo 的名字、一天几点结束、每月允许自己临时解锁几次等。

BeDo 目前不做 iCloud 同步。和其他 App 的数据一样，这些信息可能按你的 iOS 设置被包含在设备备份里（iCloud 云备份或电脑备份）。

权限和用途

屏幕使用时间

BeDo 用苹果的屏幕使用时间接口锁住你选的 App，并知道你在某个 App 里待满了约定的时间。iOS 只给 BeDo 这些 App 的加密标识，我们和 BeDo 都无法知道是哪些 App，这些标识也不会离开你的手机。

相机

用来数动作（俯卧撑、深蹲等）、拍下你留作凭证的照片、扫描要朗读的纸页。动作计数在手机上实时完成，视频不保存。扫描和图片里的文字在手机上识别。

麦克风和语音识别

用来在一件事需要录音时录下你说的话，以及在你跟 Bedo 说话时把语音转成文字。录音只存在你手机上，从不发送到任何地方。你的语言支持时，语音转文字在手机上完成；不支持时由苹果的语音识别服务处理音频，适用苹果的隐私政策。

健康

如果你把某件事设成用「健康」数据判断完成，BeDo 会读取当天的步行和跑步距离、步数、锻炼分钟数或正念分钟数。只读，从不写入「健康」。健康数据只留在你的手机上，不发给 AI 服务，不给任何人，也绝不用于广告。

照片

你选截图或图片发出去时使用。BeDo 只能看到你选中的那几张。

通知

BeDo 的通知都是在你手机上排好的本地通知，不经过任何推送服务器。

发给 AI 服务的内容

BeDo 里的伙伴 Bedo 由 AI 服务 DeepSeek（杭州深度求索人工智能基础技术研究有限公司）提供支持。第一次有内容要发出去之前，BeDo 会在对话里问你，你不同意就什么都不发。不同意也能用：自己手动建事，核对照常，Bedo 用固定的话回应；只是不提供拍照核对。

周卡和来信每件事做了几次、临时解锁了几次，以及你写过的一句话。

从不发送：你锁了哪些 App、你的录音、你的健康数据、动作计数时的视频。

照片发出前，BeDo 会先缩小并重新编码，同时去掉拍摄位置等相机信息。

请求先从你的手机发到我们放在 Cloudflare Workers 上的一个小中转服务，由它带上我们的 DeepSeek 密钥转给 DeepSeek，再把回复原样流式转回给你。中转服务不记录、不保存你发出的内容和返回的内容。为了不让这个服务被 BeDo 以外的东西拿去用，也为了数你每天说了几句，它只保存：

  • 一个匿名设备标识：你的 iPhone 用苹果的 App Attest 为 BeDo 生成的一把钥匙的编号，以及这把钥匙的公钥。它不和你的名字、Apple ID 或任何账号关联，重装 BeDo 就会换一个；90 天没用过就删除。
  • 这个设备标识今天发了几次请求（保留两天左右），以及开始之前一共发了几次。
  • 如果你是 BeDo Pro：苹果签名的购买记录的指纹（哈希值）和到期时间，省得每次都重新验苹果的签名。

和所有网络请求一样，运行中转服务的 Cloudflare 能看到你的 IP 地址，它也可能被转给 DeepSeek；中转服务不保存它。DeepSeek 在中华人民共和国境内的服务器上处理数据，保存多久由 DeepSeek 自己的隐私政策和条款决定（见 deepseek.com）。Bedo 的回复由 AI 生成，可能出错。

订阅

BeDo 由苹果销售，可以选自动续期订阅（年付带免费试用，或月付），也可以一次买断。付款由苹果处理，我们收不到你的支付信息。BeDo 通过手机上由苹果签名的购买记录判断试用、订阅或买断是否有效，并在每次 AI 请求时带上当前那一条，由我们的中转服务自己验苹果的签名；中转服务只保存这条记录的指纹和到期时间。为了防止滥用，中转服务按你的匿名设备标识计数：开始之前总共能说几句，开始之后每天有一个正常用不会碰到的上限。如果你允许通知，免费试用结束前两天 BeDo 会提醒你，这条提醒是在你手机上排好的。在 iPhone 的「设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅」里管理或取消。购买适用苹果的隐私政策。

我们会收到什么

你发给 Bedo 的内容，我们一概收不到。中转服务只保存「发给 AI 服务的内容」一节里说的匿名设备标识、每天的次数和 Pro 记录的指纹。除此之外，只有你主动发给我们的内容我们才会收到，比如邮件或 TestFlight 反馈。苹果也可能向我们提供匿名的 App Store 统计数据，以及选择了与开发者共享的用户的崩溃报告。我们只用这些来回复你、修复和改进 BeDo。

保存与删除

手机上的数据会一直保留到你删除为止。你可以删除某件事和它的记录，在「我 → 隐私与数据 → 删掉全部数据」里删除全部，或者直接删除 App。删除全部数据无法恢复。它不会重置你今天说了几句：中转服务里你那个匿名设备标识的记录，90 天没用过会自己删除。已经发给 DeepSeek 的内容按 DeepSeek 的条款保存，我们从未保存这些内容，所以无法访问或删除。

你的权利

根据你所在的地区（例如欧盟/英国的 GDPR、美国加州等州的隐私法、中国的《个人信息保护法》），你可能享有查阅、更正、删除、转移个人信息，以及反对或限制处理的权利。你的数据在你自己的手机上，在 BeDo 里就能查看、修改和删除；其他问题请联系我们。我们不出售个人信息，也不为跨情境行为广告共享个人信息。

适用 GDPR 时，我们把内容发给 AI 服务的依据是你的同意，你可以不再发送新消息并删除数据来撤回；在你手机上进行的其他处理，依据是向你提供服务。

儿童

BeDo 不面向 13 岁以下（或你所在国家法律规定的最低年龄以下）的儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的信息。

变更

如果 BeDo 发送或保存的内容有变化，我们会在变化上线前更新本页和顶部的日期；重大变化会在 App 里告诉你。
